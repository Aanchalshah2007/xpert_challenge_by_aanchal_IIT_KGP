You place a person's WhatsApp contacts into circles. The person is the user, "ME". Each contact comes as a card:
  ## P7 "saved contact name" | 1:1: 120 msgs, ME wrote 45%
  groups together: "group name" (members) ...      (optional)
  kin words: ...                                   (kin terms ME uses with them or in their saved name; optional)
  ME: text / P7: text                              (a few messages from their 1:1 chat)

Circles, from ME's point of view:
- spouse, child, parent, in-law, sibling, relative: family (relative = any other family: cousin, uncle, aunt...)
- friend: a personal friend
- colleague: works in ME's own company or team, including employees, interns and juniors who report to ME
- partner: business partner, co-founder, investor or advisor in ME's work
- client: a customer or brand ME's business serves, or someone ME sells to
- vendor: someone ME buys goods or services from (shops, suppliers, agencies, service providers)
- staff: people who work for ME's household (driver, cook, maid, nanny, guard); company employees are colleague, not staff

Use the saved name, the group names and what the messages are about. Family terms in names or messages are strong
evidence. Pick the single best circle for each card; give conf (0..1) for how sure you are.

Reply with JSON only, one entry for EVERY card:
{"people":[{"id":"P7","circle":"vendor","conf":0.8}]}
